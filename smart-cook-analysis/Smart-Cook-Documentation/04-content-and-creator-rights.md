# 04 — Content Strategy and Creator Rights

## 1. Content classes

| Source type | Description | Required provenance |
|---|---|---|
| Editorial | Produced or commissioned by Smart Cook | Author, editor, source records, asset licenses |
| Partner | Licensed from a publisher, chef, or organization | Contract, territory, term, permitted uses |
| Community | Submitted by a user creator | Creator identity/account, agreement version, asset declarations |
| AI-assisted | Created or materially transformed with AI assistance | AI-assistance disclosure, human author/editor, source inputs |

Do not label AI-assisted content as fully human-authored if that would mislead users.

## 2. Creator ownership and Smart Cook license

### Approved commercial direction
- The creator retains original ownership of content they own.
- The creator grants Smart Cook an exclusive, worldwide license covering only the rights expressly enumerated in the final agreement.
- The intended licensed uses include translation, adaptation, distribution, sublicensing, and commercial exploitation, subject to the final contract.
- The creator does not automatically receive direct payment merely for submitting content under the current Community model.
- The agreement must clearly explain the practical meaning and consequences of exclusivity.

### Legal drafting requirements
The final agreement must define:
- covered content: recipe text, photographs, video, illustrations, and other assets separately;
- exclusive rights granted and rights reserved by the creator;
- territory and duration;
- whether the creator may display the recipe in a personal portfolio or link to the Smart Cook version;
- permitted sublicensing categories and downstream obligations;
- treatment of existing sublicenses after withdrawal or termination;
- attribution and moral-rights treatment where applicable;
- warranties, infringement claims, indemnity limits, and dispute handling;
- the exact notice period and exceptions;
- what happens to backups, cached copies, and legally required records.

Do not assume every ingredient list, cooking fact, or general method is copyrightable in every jurisdiction. Protectable expression and factual information must be distinguished by qualified counsel.

## 3. Media rights are separate

The recipe license alone may not establish rights to every photograph, video, soundtrack, logo, or third-party image.

For each uploaded asset, collect:
- uploader declaration of ownership or authorization;
- asset type;
- source or license evidence where applicable;
- people/brand/property permissions where legally required;
- review status and any restrictions.

Block publication of assets with unresolved rights concerns.

## 4. Creator earnings and value exchange

Approved model: **Community** — no automatic direct payment for recipe submission.

To make this model credible, provide tangible non-cash value:
- creator profile and portfolio;
- clear attribution on every recipe;
- recipe performance analytics;
- creator badges and editorial features;
- shareable public recipe pages;
- creator tools and version history;
- transparent moderation feedback.

Do not imply revenue sharing, royalties, or guaranteed exposure unless a formal program exists.

## 5. Withdrawal and termination

Approved direction: **Notice-based withdrawal**.

The exact notice period has not yet been approved. A 30-day period was previously suggested as a discussion proposal, not a final decision.

The final policy should distinguish:
1. request received;
2. identity/ownership verification;
3. notice period;
4. removal from new public distribution;
5. treatment of existing sublicenses and partner uses;
6. backup/cache cleanup according to documented retention rules;
7. final status communicated to creator.

Urgent exceptions may be needed for infringement, safety risks, legal orders, or serious policy violations.

Do not promise immediate deletion from every backup or third-party copy unless the system and contracts can actually guarantee it.

## 6. AI training restriction — Never

Community recipe content must not be used to train, fine-tune, or improve foundation or task-specific AI models.

This restriction must cover:
- training datasets;
- fine-tuning datasets;
- evaluation datasets used to optimize model behavior where content is repurposed for training;
- vendor/provider terms and data-sharing settings.

Operational AI processing requested by the user (for example, translation, formatting, or ingredient normalization) may be considered separately, provided:
- it is necessary for the requested feature;
- the provider is contractually and technically configured not to train on submitted content;
- retention is minimized and disclosed;
- the system records the processing purpose;
- community content is not repurposed into training data.

Never send community content to an AI provider whose applicable terms or configuration permit training on that content.

## 7. Agreement evidence and versioning

Store immutable evidence for each accepted agreement:
- agreement identifier and version;
- creator/user identifier;
- content identifier and content version;
- exact rights package accepted;
- locale and displayed language;
- acceptance timestamp;
- acceptance method and relevant audit metadata;
- agreement document hash or immutable snapshot reference.

When terms change, require a new acceptance where legally necessary. Do not overwrite historical acceptance records.

## 8. Creator-facing disclosure checklist

Before submission, the user must be able to understand:
- they retain ownership of content they own;
- Smart Cook receives an exclusive worldwide license for the stated rights;
- there is no automatic direct payment under the Community model;
- content is reviewed by humans before approval;
- withdrawal is notice-based and subject to defined exceptions and existing lawful obligations;
- community content is never used to train AI models;
- images and videos require separate rights confirmation;
- translations and adaptations may be created within the granted license;
- attribution will be preserved according to policy.

## 9. Legal gate

This file is a product and engineering specification, not legal advice. Do not launch creator submission until the final agreement and privacy/consumer terms have been reviewed for the target jurisdictions by qualified counsel.
