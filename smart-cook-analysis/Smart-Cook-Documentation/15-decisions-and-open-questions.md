## 3. Important distinction: private recipe behavior

The approved rule is that private intent does not waive human review. A creator may keep an approved recipe private if the product supports that visibility, but a user-created recipe must not become an approved recipe without human review.

Drafts are not approved recipes and must not be publicly visible.

## 4. Change management

Any change to:
- exclusivity;
- creator payment;
- withdrawal;
- AI training;
- human review requirement;
- private recipe handling;
- public visibility;
must update this decision register and every affected specification.
